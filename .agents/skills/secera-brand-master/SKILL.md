## 1. When to Activate This Skill

Activate and apply this skill whenever the task involves:
- Formulating or reviewing **Brand Strategy, Positioning, or Market Role** for SECERA.
- Crafting **Website Copywriting** (Hero sections, "Inspirasi Kami" / About Us, category banners, product headlines).
- Writing **Editorial & Marketing Content** (Instagram captions, TikTok hooks, email sequences, or blog articles) adhering to SECERA's 6 Content Pillars.

- Naming new products, collections, or campaign drops according to the 3 architectural naming frameworks.

## 2. Core Identity & The 5 Golden Brand Truths

### 1. The Core Essence
> *"Tampil siap bukan soal waktu yang panjang. Tapi soal pilihan yang tepat."*  
> *"Elegance is a decision, not a process."*  
> *"Tinggal pakai. Langsung terlihat niat."*

### 2. The Category Ownership
SECERA adalah pencipta dan pemilik sah kategori **Selendang Instant #1 di Indonesia** yang sedang bertransformasi memimpin **The Effortless Occasion Wear System**.

### 3. The Tone of Voice Archetype
**Calm, Confident, Relatable, Non-Salesy, Polished, & Editorial.**  
Berbicaralah seperti sahabat paling berkelas dan bijak yang Anda miliki. Hindari nada memaksa, huruf kapital berlebihan, dan tanda seru ganda.

### 4. The Absolute Banned Words Gate
Setiap salinan teks yang dihasilkan **WAJIB BEBAS** dari kata-kata berikut:  
❌ *Murah, diskon gila, wajib checkout, gercep, auto cantik, auto glowing, viral banget, racun tiktok, anti gagal, super mewah, princess, glam maksimal, salfok, kece badai.*  
✅ *Gunakan: Investasi cerdas, harga terjangkau, siluet proporsional, anggun seketika, pilihan tepat, understated luxury.*

### 5. The "Zero Radius" Visual Doctrine
Semua komponen visual dan UI website memiliki sudut tegas 90 derajat (`border-radius: 0px`). Tidak menggunakan tombol lonjong membulat (*rounded pills*). Palet warna didominasi kanvas tenang (*Ivory/Cream/Paper*) dengan aksen signature **Brand Wine (`#722F38`)** dan tipografi **Libre Caslon Display + Inter**.

## 3. Operational Modes

### Mode A: The Brand Strategist & Marketing Director
When defining positioning, competitive moats, or campaigns:
1. Ground decisions in SECERA's 3 core personas:
   - **The Last-Minute Woman** (H-1 rush, emergency solution).
   - **The Bridesmaid Friend** (Group color coordination, zero size drama).
   - **The Polished Professional** (Office-to-dinner transition, repeat without notice).
2. Protect category ownership: Never compete solely on price against cheap marketplace imitators; compete on trust, craft, and the complete Occasion Ecosystem.
3. Consult: [references/brand_strategy_and_personas.md](references/brand_strategy_and_personas.md).

### Mode B: The Editorial & Copywriting Director
When writing customer-facing copy, headlines, or articles:
1. Apply the 4 headline formulas (Situation → Transformation, Restraint over Hype, The System over Effort, Group Harmony).
2. Align every piece of content with one of the **6 Official Content Pillars**:
   - Pillar A: *No Time, Still Stunning*
   - Pillar B: *Repeat Without Notice*
   - Pillar C: *Look Expensive*
   - Pillar D: *Real Girl Moments*
   - Pillar E: *The SECERA Effect*
   - Pillar F: *The Occasion System*
3. Consult: [references/tone_of_voice_and_lexicon.md](references/tone_of_voice_and_lexicon.md) and [references/editorial_and_content_pillars.md](references/editorial_and_content_pillars.md).

### Mode C: The Creative & Visual Art Director
When designing pages, UI sections, or prompting image generation:
1. Enforce `border-radius: 0px`, clean editorial grids, and warm dark charcoal text (`Ink #261F1F`, never `#000000`).
2. Utilize the official prompt formula for Gemini FLOW / Imagen:
   `"Editorial fashion photography of an Indonesian woman wearing SECERA [Product] in [Color], standing in a [Architectural Setting], [Natural Elegant Pose]. Soft diffused window lighting, luxury drape, 35mm style, calm confident stillness."`
3. Consult: [references/visual_system_and_art_direction.md](references/visual_system_and_art_direction.md).

## 4. The 6 Decision Filters (Self-Check Gate)

Before finalizing any brand, copy, or visual recommendation, run this mental check:
1. Does this make dressing easier for the user?
2. Does this make the wearer look more polished?
3. Does this reduce styling anxiety?
4. Does this feel effortless?
5. Does this stand out quietly with understated luxury?
6. Is this worthy of being worn in life's most meaningful moments?
